What it is

A working studio system, owned by the people who make and watch the films.

INTERDEPENDENT is a film studio organized as a member-owned LLC. It runs a script-to-screen production system — with the departments you'd find at a legacy major — paired with an online platform that lets writers, directors, crew, and audiences participate from anywhere.

Productions are greenlit using real signal: filmmakers willing to attach for equity, and audiences willing to pay before the film exists. Every contributor — screenwriter, director, department head, crew, executive producer — can hold a real economic stake in the films they help make.

What we're building

Five surfaces, one studio.

A platform, a campus, an audience, an attribution model, and the events that turn each film into something worth showing up for.

01

The Lot

An online development, pre-production, and hybrid traditional/generative production facility. Where the work gets made — together, in the open.

02

Role-based attribution

Every contribution — writing, directing, crew, capital, IP — is documented and rewarded through a Production Interest in that film.

03

The Mailroom

A global readership and audience layer. Screenplays read by real people; pre-sales and feedback that feed greenlight decisions.

04

Future-Proof Production Campus

A dual-use traditional and AI/compute facility on existing fiber backbone in Coudersport, Pennsylvania.

05

Event-driven distribution

Readings, premieres, retail-partner screenings, and screen-used collector drops — films as occasions, not just streams.

06

One studio, one ladder

Economics governed by a published Operating Agreement and Economic Attribution Policy. The same rules for everyone, every film.

Who it's for

Filmmakers, audiences, and crew — on the same side of the table.

FILMMAKERS

Writers and directors without industry connections who'd rather earn equity than chase paychecks. Bring the story; we provide the system to make it.

CREW & CRAFTSPEOPLE

Department heads and craftspeople who want to be partners in the productions they work on, not line items on a budget.

AUDIENCES

People who want to read, vote on, and help shape the films they'll eventually pay to see — and share in the upside of the ones that work.

Why

The studio system stopped making the greatest films of all time.

Major studios bet on franchises. Streamers optimize for retention. Neither rewards conviction.

INTERDEPENDENT exists to put filmmakers, audiences, and crew on the same side of the table — risk-aligned, economically attached, and in shared pursuit of films worth showing up for.
